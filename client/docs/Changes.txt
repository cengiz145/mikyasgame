New in 4.0:
Important announcement: it is now possible to follow the deaths and other information of Desafio Mortal from the Twitter account @megadimensao!
There are new maps! and the old maps have been modify!
Launched the time machine! And with it, a new world to be explored and a new object to be used.
New vehicles! you can now ride the bus and on the boat liner!
And speaking of vehicles, in case your connection drops when you are inside of one, you will die automatically. That is, when you return to the game you'll be in
the tunnel. But don't worry! All players
will be back to where you died when you come out of the tunnel. And all the bodies will also be found where the death happened.
New enemies! There are animals walking around the maps, ready to attack or be attacked.
New games in the casino! Black Jack and Farkle. Ah, and now the casino is in a new location.
New fruits! Now you have many alternatives to raise your health. And with that, we bring two modifications 
In case your connection drops and you return to the game within the time limit of 10 minutes, your health will continue as it was, instead of the 5000 by default.
And speaking of health, the maximum that a player can get will be of 25000 points.
Good news for players who enjoy fighting as a team! Now the xp will be divided among the members. And in case you are 5 steps away they will be healed when one 
of them press the letter U.
We modified the way to become a pacifist! Search and discover for yourself.
Now most objects contain descriptions, which you can read by pressing CTRL+Enter on the inventory.
Launched the translator, using Google translation to translate messages instantly. To activate and deactivate, change the language of origin, among other functions,
go to the profile settings.
Messages preceded by the less-than sign "<" will not be translated.
Added the option to Disable Chat in the menu F6. And now, when you open it, the cursor will be automatically positioned on the last element visited.
Modified the function of the numbers. Now, it will be used for your favourite weapons! Just select a weapon in the  inventory and press SHIFT+a number
in which you want to configure the gun.
Speaking of weapons, you can see how many ammunition is in the weapon selected with the letter X, total ammunition  with SHIFT+X and reload the selected weapon 
with SHIFT+R. thanks goes to the player Loushin for the idea.
Added the key commands SHIFT+page down and SHIFT+page up to increase and decrease the volume of the game. Also added the command SHIFT+F3 to activate and deactivate 
the announcements of the game in other windows.
The ask for help key has been change, now its the letter f. some keyboards didn't have the key used before.
The keys, comma, dot, semicolon, open bracket, close bracket, shift-comma and shift dot can now be used anywhere in the game. For example: within the inventory.
Removed the voice Defensor. Sadly we could not get updates of the audios, so we had to remove it.
Some maps now have different sounds for the day and night.
the echo of the steps when the player is within 6 steps of falling from a platform has been removed, and added a slip sound for when the fall happens.
Now coordinate and the camera can be disabled on some maps.
Bullets more realistic! Now the bullets are fast... Very fast!
The damage that guns cause was balanced.


Bugs fixed:
before it was possible to load the weapon and shoot, even loos ammo, when still being a newbie but the shots did not reach the target. That doesn't happen anymore. 
Thanks the player Crauser for telling us about it.
If you disconnect from the game paralyzed,  will come back in the same way.
you can't use the item_magnet going down anymore
you can't get out of the maps before you reach your final destination when you're in vehicles!.
The spell repressed  heat now protects you from fire.
The chat now does not repeat the letters beeing written.
the items don't float in the air anymore.
The message when you connect and disconnect doesn't show anymore.
Finally! Fixed bug with sounds that move when you exit a map and return.
